Ropa Verde

ProteinSteak
CarbsPotato
VegetablesBell Pepper, Garlic, Tomato, White Onion
FatOlive Oil
GarnishCumin, Oregano, Red Pepper, Tomato Paste

Cut a chuck steak (?) into cubes and season them with salt generously. Sear them in olive oil (preheat pan and oil on medium). Remove and set aside when they are browned.

Remove the fat. Julienne onions, bell peppers, and serrano peppers with some garlic and add them to the pot/pan and sear. Add tomato paste, salt, cumin, and oregano. When they become floppy, deglaze with red wine and beef stock. Add back the steak and simmer until tender. Add salt and bay leaves/herbs to taste. Add beans toward in the end.
