Russian Salad

⭐⭐⭐⭐

CarbsPotato
VegetablesCarrot, Pickle
FatMayo
Ingredients
  • 4-5 large potatoes
  • Carrots - a small bag of small cleaned carrots
  • Frozen or canned peas
  • Pickles - one medium size jar
  • A small jar of mayonnaise
Method

Rinse the potatoes and boil them until soft. Peel the skin and dice them in small pieces. Boil the carrots until soft and then dice in small pieces Chop the pickles in small pieces and squeeze excess water from them. Drain the water from canned peas or if using frozen peas - add some oil and a couple of tablespoons of water in a pot and saute the peas. Mix the diced potatoes, carrots, pickles and peas and add the mayo. Mix everything together. You can add the juice of half a lemon.and black paper
