CarbsLentils
VegetablesCarrot, Celery, Garlic, White Onion
FatOlive Oil
GarnishBay Leaf, Chubritsa, Tomato Paste
HeatSimmering
Ingredients
  • 1 pack of lentils
  • Savory
  • Salt
  • 1 Onion
  • 2-3 small pieces of garlic
  • Carrots
  • 3 teaspoons of oil
  • 1 small can of tomato sauce or 1-2 fresh tomatoes
Method

Chop the onion, carrot and garlic into small pieces. In a medium pot fill up water and add the <u>rinsed</u> lentils, the chopped onion, garlic and carrots. Once it starts boiling, turn down the heat and simmer. Add 2-3 tablespoons of oil. Simmer for about 30 min, then add the chubritsa, 1 tablespoon salt and some tomato sauce. Continue simmering until fully cooked. Stir occasionally.
