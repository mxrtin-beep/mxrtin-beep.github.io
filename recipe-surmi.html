Surmi

⭐⭐⭐

ProteinGround Lamb
CarbsRice
VegetablesCabbage Leaves, White Onion
FatOlive Oil
GarnishChubritsa, Cumin
HeatBraising
Ingredients
  • Jar of cabbage leaves
  • Rice
  • Onion
  • Savory
  • Cumin
  • Ground pork or lamb
  • Oil
Method

In a medium pot put 2-3 tablespoons of oil and add chopped onion. Saute on the stove at medium heat. Then add the rinsed rice and 1 and ½ cups of water. Bring to a boil and simmer for 5-6 min. Remove from the heat and add the ground meat, savory, cumin seed anymix really well. In a large pot lay some onion or cabbage leaves on the bottom. Stuff the cabbage leaves and arrange in the pot. At the end mix the cabbage juice with some water and add to the pot. Cover with a small lid so that it snuggly covers the stuffed cabbage. Cover the pot with its own lid. Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat. Simmer for about 40 min.
