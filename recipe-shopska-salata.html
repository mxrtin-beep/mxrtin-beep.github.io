Shopska Salata

VegetablesCucumber, Red Onion, Tomato
FatOlive Oil
DairyFeta
GarnishVinegar
Ingredients
  • Tomatoes
  • Cucumbers
  • Sweet peppers
  • Onion
  • Parsley
  • Feta cheese
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
Method

Chop and mix all ingredients. Add some salt and oil to taste. Garnish with grated feta cheese.
