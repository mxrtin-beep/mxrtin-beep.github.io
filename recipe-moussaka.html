Moussaka

⭐⭐⭐

ProteinEgg, Ground Lamb
CarbsFlour, Potato
VegetablesWhite Onion
FatButter
DairyMilk
GarnishParsley
HeatBaking
Ingredients
  • Potatoes
  • Ground pork or lamb
  • Onion
  • Parsley
  • 1 egg
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Cumin
  • Paprika
Method

Peel and wash the potatoes and chop them into small square pieces. Chop 1 onion. Mix the chopped potatoes, onion, ground meat and some parsley. Add some salt, cumin and paprika. Mix well. Spread 1-2 tablespoons of oil on a large baking pan. Pour the mix and spread evenly. Bake covered with aluminum foil for about 30 min. Mix well again and continue baking uncovered. To make Bechamel Sauce: In saucepan, melt butter over medium-low heat; whisk in flour and cook slowly until smooth and lightly cooked (2-3 minutes); add salt and remove from heat; add milk, whisking rapidly and return to heat; cook until thick and bubbly, whisking constantly; allow to cool 5 minutes, whisking once in a while to prevent a skin from forming; incorporate eggs with wire whisk to make fluffy sauce. Pour the sauce over the moussaka once potatoes are cooked and bake until golden brown.
