Braised Pork Ribs

⭐⭐⭐⭐

ProteinPork Ribs
CarbsPotato
VegetablesCarrot, Celery, Garlic, White Onion
FatOlive Oil
GarnishBasil, Bay Leaf, Oregano, Tomato Paste, Vinegar

Salt the pork ribs the day before. Heat up enough pan/pot space for the ribs on medium. Add a bit of olive oil when it’s hot. When the oil is hot enough, add the ribs fat down (they should sizzle). Flip them when they unstick. Brown all sides.

While the ribs are browning, chop up white onion, carrots, and celery. When the ribs are done, set them aside and remove the fat in the pan. Add the chopped onions, carrots, and celery and brown them. Cook with salt and paprika/oregano. After a bit, deglaze with red wine or stock and scrape the fond. Combine all the vegetables into a pot. Add the pork ribs on top and add enough water, stock, or wine to halfway up the ribs. Braise on low for a few hours.

While braising, cube a potato and add it on top. Add thyme, garlic, salt, vinegar/lemon juice, and/or rosemary.

You’ll know it’s ready when the meat pulls apart easily with two forks.
