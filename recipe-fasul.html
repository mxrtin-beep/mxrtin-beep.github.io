Fasul

CarbsNorthern Beans
VegetablesCarrot, Celery
FatOlive Oil
DairyYogurt
GarnishBay Leaf, Chubritsa, Paprika, Tomato Paste
Ingredients
  • 1 pack of small white beans
  • Carrots
  • Savory
  • Spearmint
  • Salt
  • Paprika
  • 1 Onion
  • 3 tablespoons of oil
  • 1 small can of tomato sauce or 1-2 fresh tomatoes
Method

In a medium pot soak the beans overnight in water. The next day drain the water, fill up fresh water to cover the beans and bring to a boil. Once it is boiling, drain the water and add fresh water and bring to a boil again. Repeat 2-3 times. Continue simmering the beans for about an hour with 2-3 tablespoons of oil . Then add the chopped carrots, onion, savory and spearmint. Boil for another 15 - 30 min. Towards the end add the tomato sauce and 1 tablespoon of salt and some paprika.
