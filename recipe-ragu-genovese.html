Ragu Genovese

ProteinBeef Shank
CarbsPasta
VegetablesCarrot, Celery, Chard, Red Onion
FatButter, Olive Oil
GarnishBlack Pepper, Oregano, Parmesan, Red Wine Vinegar

Cut up some carrots and celery and brown them in a pot with salt. When they are slightly browned, remove them and add diced, pre-salted beef (ideally salted the day before). Brown. Deglaze with water or red wine. Don’t add too much liquid, only to get to about halfway up the meat. Add back the carrots and celery. Add julienned onions on top with salt, black pepper, and oregano. Simmer for a few hours. You’ll know it’s ready when the onions are mushy and the meat falls apart easily.

When it’s about ready, cook some chard in a pan with hot olive oil or butter. Add a bunch of chard because they will shrink. When they’re ready, add them to the pot and add the pasta to the pan. Toast it for a bit before adding water and scraping off the chard fond. Boil the pasta in the pan until ready and add to the pot. Delicious!!!!
