Garlic Butter Chicken Pasta

ProteinChicken Breast
CarbsPasta
VegetablesGarlic
FatButter, Olive Oil
GarnishBlack Pepper, Red Pepper

Salt chicken breast the night before (optional).

Preheat stainless steel pan on medium. When it’s hot enough, add fat (I used solid coconut oil). After the oil heats up, add the chicken. It should start sizzling and stick to the pan.

While the chicken is cooking, boil pasta in salty water. Turn the heat off when it’s 3/4 of the way cooked.

When the chicken unsticks, flip it. Add extra oil if necessary. Cook the second side until it unsticks. After both sides of the chicken are cooked, remove the chicken and set it aside.

Cool the pan down. Add olive oil, a few cloves of garlic, and red pepper flakes. Keep on low heat. The garlic should bubble and fizzle to infuse into the olive oil but not cook.

After a few minutes, add the pasta back in with pasta water. Before, make sure the oil isn’t so hot that adding the pasta causes the oil to splash. Add the chicken as well.

Once the pasta is in, add a good amount of pasta water. Heat the pan enough for the water to evaporate. Add butter and stir continuously, letting the pasta water reduce and stick to the pasta. Scrape the chicken fond into the pasta water as you stir. Add black pepper and lemon juice or vinegar to taste.

After the water is reduced enough, turn off the heat and serve.
