Banitsa

⭐⭐⭐⭐

ProteinEgg
CarbsPhyllo Dough
FatButter
DairyFeta, Milk
GarnishSesame Seed
HeatBaking
Ingredients
  • Phyllo Dough
  • Feta Cheese
  • 3 Large Eggs
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Sesame seed
Method

Thaw the frozen Phyllo Dough by taking out of the package and leaving outside for about an hour. Mix eggs and cheese in a bowl. Melt some butter in the microwave. Put some oil on your baking pan and sprinkle some flour on it. Preheat the oven to 350-360 degrees. Put down some sheets on the pan and alternate spreading the cheese mix and melted butter. At the end, cut the pieces and pour some milk on top. Sprinkle some sesame seed. Bake for about 35 min or until golden.
