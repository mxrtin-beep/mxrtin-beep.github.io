Gyuveche

ProteinEgg
VegetablesMushroom, Olive, Tomato
FatButter
DairyFeta
GarnishParsley
Ingredients
  • Clay pot
  • Feta cheese
  • Tomatoes or tomato sauce
  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Olives
  • Mushrooms (or veggies)
  • Parsley
Method

In the clay pot start with some butter, chopped tomatoes or tomato sauce. Add any chopped vegetable or mushrooms. Add blocks of feta cheese. Add some olives and top with two eggs. Cover the clay pot and bake at 400 degrees for about 20-  30 min.
